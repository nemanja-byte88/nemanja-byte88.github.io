Our story

Slow days, long tables.

Aurevia is a handful of stone houses on a quiet hillside, looked after by one family. Breakfast comes from the garden, evenings end under the pergola, and the hills do the rest.

There are only a few rooms, so we know every guest by name. Come for a weekend or stay for a week — children and grandparents are equally at home here.

Questions

Often asked.

Can we arrive without a car?

Yes. We can arrange a transfer from the airport or the train station; add it in the booking step.

Is breakfast included?

Breakfast is served on the terrace every morning and can be added to any stay.

Do you welcome small children?

Very much so. The garden, the pool and the little house were all planned with families in mind.
