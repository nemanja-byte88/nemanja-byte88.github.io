How we press our oil

The olives are picked by hand in late autumn and pressed within hours, so the oil keeps its green colour and a peppery finish. We bottle in dark glass to protect it from light.

A plate of tomatoes and bread

Thick slices of ripe tomato, good bread, a pinch of salt and a generous pour of olive oil. Nothing else is needed. Eat it outside if you can.

Honey and lavender, side by side

Our bees visit the lavender rows in early summer. The honey that comes from it is light, floral and lovely on warm bread or stirred into yoghurt.

Our estate

Small on purpose.

Everything in the pantry comes from the hills around Aurevia, a small family-run stay in Tuscany. We make little, and we make it carefully.
